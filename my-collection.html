About My Collection

I hold my stamp collection in a Lindener stockbook that I purchased from an online hobby shop. More formal collectors often choose albums with custom or pre-made mounts, but I prefer my stockbook; it allows me to move things around whenever I may need and organize the collection however I choose.

I organize my stamps by geography, with pages dedicated to a particular country or region of the world. In total, there are about 74 countries represented in my collection!

Collection Highlights

A few of my favorite stamps from my collection, either for their memories, their art, or their history:

The postal service has a long history of celebrating philately and helping out stamp collectors! This stamp commemorates the hobby and historical American stamps.

This Rush themed stamp came from a sheet of four, each dedicated to an iconic Canadian band. I had to split up the sheet to gift my dad the one for the Tragically Hip, one of his favorite artists.

There's no particular reason I included this stamp from Germany except that I just really like trains! And I especially love seering trains on postage!

One of the oldest stamps in my collection. Right after the Bolshevik Revolution, before there was the CCCP, there was the РСФСР, or the Russian Socialist Federal Soviet Republic.

I got this stamp in person at Shakespeare's Birthplace in Stratford-Upon-Avon when I visited in 2023, the 400th anniversary of the publishing of the First Folio.

An example of how beautiful postage stamp art can be. This example is from Mongolia from 1979 to commemorate the International Year of the Child.

This stamp features a reproduction of the first original stamp design issued by the Irish Free State following independence. The choice to print an image of a unified Ireland (especially in 1972) shows how postage stamps can be political actors.

Fun fact! The United Nations has produced their own stamps since 1951!
